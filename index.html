About

I am a digital archivist and author in Ibadan, Nigeria. I have written nine books on Yoruba lineage and digital genealogy, and I developed the Ilefoastro Method, a simple way for families to record and keep their own oral history.

The Ilefoastro Method

Three steps for documenting a family's history:

  • 1. Record. Interview elders, with their permission, and note who told you each fact and when.
  • 2. Check. Compare each account with documents, archives and other relatives. Mark it Pending until a second source supports it, then Verified.
  • 3. Preserve. Keep the record in an organised digital file, with copies in more than one place.

The Method helps families organise their own oral history. It does not prove descent, rank or chieftaincy titles, and it does not replace government, court or palace records.
